What Ralph does

Start with the real problem.

Ralph is most useful when the question is specific: compare two televisions inside an actual room, tune equipment around a real system, turn exam prep into adaptive coaching, or chase down a product with one non-negotiable constraint.

He carries context, changes methods when the first approach misses, and helps turn a vague need into something concrete enough to use.
