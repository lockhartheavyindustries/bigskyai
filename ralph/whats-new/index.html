  1. July 28, 2026

    New skill: “Whassup with…”

    Ralph can now give you a quick snapshot of what people are saying on X about a current news story, sports event, public figure, company, product launch, or other trending topic. Text him whassup with <topic>—for example, whassup with the World Cup? or whassup with the latest iPhone launch?—and about a minute later he'll summarize what happened, why people are discussing it, the major viewpoints, and notable posts you can read for yourself.

    Be sure to spell it “whassup” so Ralph recognizes the request. It works for public topics—not personal questions—and it reflects public conversation on X, which may include opinions, rumors, or incorrect claims.
