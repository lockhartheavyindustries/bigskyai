Built for day one

Guitar practice without the panic.

Most beginners do not need a wall of songs or a score that makes them feel behind. Strummer starts smaller: one acoustic guitar, one iPad, one friendly practice session that helps a new player form chords and strum in time.

What it teaches

A simple loop beginners can actually finish.

1

Finger placement that feels visible

Strummer uses the iPad camera view and clear overlays to help a learner see the chord shape before trying to play it.

2

Feedback that does not overclaim

Instead of brittle scores, the app keeps feedback broad and useful: sounds right, not quite, late, or try slower.

3

Short sessions with a real ending

The first path is built around Em, G, C, and D, with practice that wraps up before the beginner burns out.

First session

Ten minutes, start to recap.

The app is designed to guide a beginner through a complete seated practice moment: setup, tuning check, chord shape, slow strum, two-chord change, and a small recap.

00:00

Set up the guitar view

Place the iPad, confirm acoustic guitar, and align the fretboard guides.

02:00

Learn one shape

Start with a calm chord placement prompt before any timing pressure appears.

05:00

Strum with a slow pulse

Use the metronome to build rhythm at a pace that still feels human.

09:00

End with next steps

Finish with what improved and one gentle suggestion for the next practice.

The promise

Less like a test. More like someone sitting beside you.

Strummer keeps the early guitar experience forgiving, local-first, and focused on the small physical wins that make a beginner want to come back tomorrow.
