The difference

A camera-guided coach for your hands.

Strummer is built around the moment other apps miss: what your fingers are actually doing on the guitar. The iPad camera watches your hand position, the app shows where your fingers should go, and audio feedback helps confirm what you played.

What it teaches

Realtime feedback before bad habits set in.

1

Hand tracking on the actual guitar

The core experience is a live camera view of the learner's fretting hand, aligned to the guitar so guidance happens where practice happens.

2

Ghost-hand placement guidance

Visual overlays can show where each finger belongs for beginner chord shapes, making the invisible part of practice easier to copy.

3

Audio feedback for notes and timing

The microphone listens alongside the camera, helping the app respond to notes played, strums, rhythm, and slow beginner chord changes.

First session

Ten minutes, start to recap.

The app is designed to guide a beginner through a complete seated practice moment: setup, tuning check, chord shape, slow strum, two-chord change, and a small recap.

00:00

05:00
